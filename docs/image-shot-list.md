# Twelve photographs

House frame, every shot: **dark ground, tight crop, single dish, no prop styling.**
The design brief's own line. Anything that reads as food-blog styling — linen,
scattered herbs, a hand reaching in — fights the register.

Drop them at `public/images/recipes/` using exactly these filenames. Landscape,
at least 1200px on the long edge. `FigureWell` crops with `object-fit: cover` at
440×520, so the subject wants to survive a portrait crop.

## Two that carry more weight than the rest

**041 and 032** should read as the same idea at two scales — a bird and a
vegetable under the same press. The learner's whole page argues that those are one
technique, and if the photographs make that visible without a caption, the strongest
page in the demo gets stronger for free.

## Where images do and do not appear

Only at `full` treatment, and no more than two `full` blocks per composition. So on
any given page you will see one or two of these, not twelve. Six or seven good ones
would carry the whole demo if sourcing twelve is a squeeze — prioritise
041, 032, 038, 039, 036, 035.
